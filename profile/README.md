# MetaboliQ

Engineering the foundations of intelligence.

We engineer, deploy and operate AI systems that solve real business problems. Our customer engineering work covers AI workflows, custom agents, integrations and production operations.

## How we approach the work

- Start with a defined workflow and an agreed baseline.
- Evaluate representative tasks and failure cases.
- Make permissions, human oversight and operational ownership explicit.
- Use reproducible experiments to guide technical decisions.

## The long view

Compute, Models and Silicon are our long-term research and investment directions. Public work should make its scope, evidence and limitations clear.
